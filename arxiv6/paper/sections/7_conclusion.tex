\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented \method{}, a lightweight multimodal framework for real-time instrument classification on public performance videos. Rather than evaluating the system only through clean-set accuracy, the paper examined classification quality together with inference budget and robustness to cross-modal mismatch and input degradation. On the evaluated URMP and Solos setup, \method{} achieves the strongest overall balance among Macro-F1, latency, and robustness relative to the lightweight baselines considered in this study. Future work will extend the evaluation to broader datasets, stronger supervision regimes, and repeated-seed statistical reporting, including richer event-level annotations where public data permits.
